# Boards

A **board** is a set of columns that hold cards. Each card is one thing to
do. Use a board for the work left on a book, or for anything else you want to
follow.

A card can have a due date, a priority, a checklist and links to your
documents. You can also put a checklist item from a note on a board. Then the
item and its card change together: tick the item, and the card moves.

## Make a board

1. Go to the place for boards:
   - In Notes, open **Boards** under **Places** in the sidebar.
   - In Write, open the [project](./collections.md) the board is for, and choose
     **Board** under it.
2. Press **New tasks board** or **New custom board**. In a project, click the
   list's **+**, then **New tasks board for this project**.
3. Name the board.
4. Press **Add card** at the bottom of a column, and type what you need to
   do.

There are two kinds of board:

| Kind | Columns | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tasks board** | **To do**, **Doing**, **Done** | Things to finish. The first column means "to do". The last column means "done". Every column between them means "in progress". Only a tasks board can track checklist items. |
| **Custom board** | **Column 1**, **Column 2**, **Column 3** | Anything else. You name the columns and decide what they mean. |

**+** at the top of the Boards list also makes a tasks board. The list's
**⋮** has **New custom board**.

## Which boards you see

- **In Write**, a board belongs to a project. Open the project and choose
  **Board**. Write's sidebar has no Boards place. A project's board always
  opens in its project, even when you open it from `⌘K`, a card link, or
  **Due this week** on Home.
- **In Notes**, **Boards** under **Places** holds the boards that belong to
  no project.

When no board is open, the page shows each board as a tile. A tile shows the
board's columns and the number of open cards. Click a tile to open the board.

## Work with cards

Drag a card to move it to another column, or up and down in its column.
Click a card to open it. An open card has these fields:

<img src="/img/app/board-card-light.png" alt="An open board card with its notes, checklist, linked chapter, due date, priority and label" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<img src="/img/app/board-card-dark.png" alt="An open board card with its notes, checklist, linked chapter, due date, priority and label" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** and **Notes** | What you need to do, and a description. |
| **Checklist** | Steps. Type a step and press Return. |
| **Linked to** | Documents, characters and projects. Click a link to open it. |
| **Column** | The column the card is in. |
| **Due** | A date. The card appears on the [calendar](./calendar.md) on that day, and under **Due this week** on Write's Home. |
| **Remind** | A date and time. |
| **Priority** | **Low**, **Normal**, **High** or **Urgent**. |
| **Label** | A colour, or **No label**. |

There are two ways to remove a card from the board:

- **Archive card** hides the card but does not delete it.
- **Delete card** moves the card to the trash. **Restore** in the trash puts
  it back in its column.

## Arrange the columns

- **Rename**: click a column's name and type.
- **Colour**: click the dot beside the name and choose a colour, or **No
  colour**.
- **Move**: drag a column by its handle. On a tasks board the order matters:
  the first column is "to do" and the last is "done".
- **Add**: choose **Add column**, at the end of the columns or in the board's
  **⋮**.
- **Remove**: click the **×** on an empty column. If the column has cards,
  move them first. A tasks board always keeps at least two columns.

## Keep a board tidy

The board's header shows the number of open cards, a **Match a word…** field,
and **Show archived**.

The board's **⋮** has:

- **Hide what is done**: hides the cards in the last column.
- **Archive what is done**: archives every card in the last column at once.
- **Add column**.
- **Board settings**: here you can change the board's **Name**, its
  **Project** (choose **Standalone** to show the board in Notes) and its
  columns. **Delete board** moves the board to the trash.

Archived cards are not shown and not counted, but they stay in the board's
file. **Show archived** shows them again, struck through. To return an
archived card to the board, open it and choose **Put back on the board**.

## Track a checklist item on a board

1. In a note, write a checklist item: type `[]` or use `/checklist`.
2. Open the [Info panel](./finding-your-way.md#the-info-panel) (`⇧⌘I`) and
   choose **Tasks**. It lists every checklist item in the note.
3. Press **Track** beside an item. Choose a tasks board, or make a new one.

φ Poiesis makes a card that links back to the note. The item and the card now change
together:

- Tick the item, and the card moves to the last column.
- Move the card, and the item changes to match.

If the item has a nested list under it, that list becomes the card's
checklist.

Notes' Home lists the **Open action items** from your Notes boards, with a
link to **All boards**.

:::note

You can start new checklists in notes and research pages. To start them in
Write or Journal too, open **Settings → Setup**, find the mode under
**Modes**, and turn on **Checklists**. A document that already has a
checklist always keeps it.

:::

## Put a whole document on a board

1. Click the document's **⋮**, then **Add to board…**. The same command is at
   the bottom of the Info panel's **Outline** tab.
2. Give the card a title.
3. Choose a tasks board.

The card links to the document. The document's **Details…** page lists the
card.

## A project's chapter board

Every project has one board that Poiesis makes for you. It shows the project's
chapters as cards, in four status columns: **Todo**, **Draft**, **Revised**
and **Final**. Drag a card to another column, and the chapter's status
changes.

<img src="/img/app/chapter-board-light.png" alt="A project's chapter board, with the chapters as cards in one column per status" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<img src="/img/app/chapter-board-dark.png" alt="A project's chapter board, with the chapters as cards in one column per status" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

To open it, open the project and choose **Board** under it in the sidebar.

The list beside the page shows the project's boards. The chapter board (or
poem board), **By status**, comes first. The boards you made for the project
come after it. Click a board in the list to show it on the page. Its cards
open on the same page.
