# Boards

A **board** is columns of cards: the jobs left on a book, the threads to
follow up, anything you want to keep moving. A card can carry a due date, a
priority, a checklist and links to your documents. A checklist item in a note
can live on a board too, and ticking it in one place moves it in the other.

## Make a board

1. Open **Boards** under **Places** in the sidebar. In Write, open the project
   the board is for first.
2. Press **New tasks board** or **New custom board**.
3. Name the board.
4. Press **Add card** at the foot of a column, and type what needs doing.

| Kind | Columns | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tasks board** | **To do**, **Doing**, **Done** | Things to finish. The first column means to do, the last means done, and anything between is in progress. Only a tasks board can track checklist items. |
| **Custom board** | **Column 1**, **Column 2**, **Column 3** | Anything else. The columns mean whatever you name them. |

**+** at the top of the Boards list makes a tasks board too, and the list's
**⋮** has **New custom board**.

## Which boards you see

Each mode keeps its own boards:

- **Write** shows the boards that belong to a project.
- **Notes** shows the boards that belong to none.

With no board open, the page shows each board as a tile: its columns and how
many cards are open. Click one to open it.

## Work with cards

Drag cards between columns, and up and down. Click a card to open it:

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** and **Notes** | What needs doing, and a description. |
| **Checklist** | Steps: type one and press Return. |
| **Linked to** | Documents, characters and projects. Click a link to open it. |
| **Column** | Where the card sits. |
| **Due** | A date. The card shows on the [calendar](./calendar) that day, and under **Due this week** on Write's Home. |
| **Remind** | A date and time. |
| **Priority** | **Low**, **Normal**, **High** or **Urgent**. |
| **Label** | A colour, or **No label**. |

**Archive card** puts it away without deleting it. **Delete card** moves it to
the trash; **Restore** there puts it back in its column.

## Arrange the columns

- **Rename**: click a column's name and type.
- **Colour**: click the dot beside the name, or **No color**.
- **Move**: drag a column by its handle. On a tasks board the order is the
  meaning, first to do and last done.
- **Add**: **Add column**, at the end of the columns or in the board's **⋮**.
- **Remove**: the **×** on an empty column. Move its cards first. A tasks
  board keeps at least two columns.

## Keep a board tidy

The board's header shows how many cards are open, a **Match a word…** field,
and **Show archived**. Its **⋮** has:

- **Hide what is done**: keeps the last column's cards out of view.
- **Archive what is done**: puts every card in the last column away at once.
  Archived cards stop showing and counting, but stay in the board's file.
  **Show archived** brings them back struck through, and a card's **Put back
  on the board** returns it.
- **Add column**.
- **Board settings**: the board's **Name**; its **Project**, or
  **Standalone** to show it in Notes; its columns; and **Delete board**, which
  moves it to the trash.

## Track a checklist item on a board

1. In a note, write a checklist item: type `[]` or use `/checklist`.
2. Open the Info panel (`⇧⌘I`) and choose **Tasks**. Every checklist item in
   the note is listed.
3. Press **Track** beside one, and choose a tasks board, or make one.

The card links back to the note, and the two stay in step: tick the item and
the card moves to the last column; move the card and the item follows. An
item with a nested list takes it along, as the card's checklist.

Notes' Home lists the **Open action items** from your Notes boards, with a
link to **All boards**.

:::note

New checklists can be started in notes and research pages. To start them in
Write or Journal as well, open **Settings → Setup**, find the mode under
**Modes**, and turn on **Checklists**. A document that already has a
checklist always keeps it.

:::

## Put a whole document on a board

Open the document's **⋮** → **Add to board…** (also at the foot of the Info
panel's **Outline** tab). Give the card a title and pick a tasks board. The
card links to the document, and the document's **Details…** lists the card.

## A project's chapter board

Every project has a board you never have to keep. Open the project and choose
**Board** under it in the sidebar, or **Open project board** from its **⋮**.
It shows the chapters as cards in status columns, **Todo**, **Draft**,
**Revised** and **Final**. Drag a card and the chapter's status changes. The
project's tasks boards are listed below it, with **New tasks board for this
project**. See [Projects](./collections).
