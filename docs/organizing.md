# Organizing

φ Poiesis gives you four simple tools to organize your documents: folders, tags,
stars, and a sort for every list. They change only how you find a document,
never how it is written. Use as many or as few as you like.

## File a document in a folder

1. Open **Pieces** in Write, or a folder in Notes.
2. If you need a new folder, click **New folder** at the bottom of the list
   and name it.
3. Drag the document onto the folder.
4. Click the folder to open it.

The folder path above the list (**All › Drafts › Poems**) shows where you
are. Click a step in the path to go back to it.

## Pieces and notes

Chapters, journal days and morning pages have their own places. Every other
document is one of two kinds:

- **Pieces** are in Write, under **Pieces**. A piece is a poem, an essay or a
  story outside any project. A piece can have a status and a word goal. You
  can add it to a project at any time (see [Projects](./collections.md)).
- **Notes** are in Notes. A note is an idea, a source or a line you
  overheard. Notes have no status and no word goal (see
  [Notes & capture](./notes.md)).

Pieces and notes share the same folders. Notes shows every folder. Pieces
shows the folders that contain pieces, and any new, empty folders.

## Folders

You do not have to use folders. A document in no folder is shown at the top
of the list.

### Make a folder

There are four ways:

- Click **New folder** at the bottom of a folder listing. The new folder is
  made inside the folder you are looking at.
- Click **+** beside **Folders** in the Notes sidebar.
- Click the list's **⋮**, then **New folder** (in Write or Notes).
- Right-click a folder, then choose **New folder inside**.

Poiesis asks you to **Name the new folder**.

### A folder's menu

Right-click a folder in the Notes sidebar or in a list:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **New piece** / **New note here** | Makes a new document in this folder. Write shows **New piece**; Notes shows **New note here**. |
| **New folder inside** | Makes a folder inside this one. |
| **Rename this folder** | Gives it a new name. |
| **Icon…** | Chooses an icon for it (see below). |
| **Colour** | **None**, **Grey**, **Red**, **Orange**, **Yellow**, **Green**, **Blue**, **Purple** or **Pink**. The folder's icon takes this colour. |
| **Delete this folder** | Removes the folder, after asking you. Its documents are not deleted; they move to the top level. You cannot undo this. |

### Icons and colours

A folder or a project can have its own icon. Right-click it and choose
**Icon…**. For the project you are in, the list's **⋮** also has **Icon…**
and **Colour…**.

The icon picker has a search field, a row of colours, and icons in groups.
**Default** brings back the plain folder or book icon.

The icon and colour appear everywhere the folder or project is named: the
sidebar, the list, the folder path and Home.

## Move a document

| To move it | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Into a folder | Drag it onto the folder, in a list or in the Notes sidebar. Or right-click it and choose **Move to folder**. Or choose the folder under **In** on its **Details…** page. |
| Out of every folder | Drop it on **All** in the folder path, or choose **No folder**. |
| Up a level | Drop it on a step of the folder path. |
| Into a different order | Drop it between two other documents. The order is kept while the list is sorted by **Edited**. |
| From Notes to Write, or back | Right-click it and choose **Move to Write's pieces** or **Move to Notes**. The same choice is on the document's **⋮** and on its Details page. |
| Into a project | Right-click a piece and choose **Add to project** or **Move to project**. Or choose **Add it to a project…** on its Details page. A note moves to Write's pieces first. |
| Out of a project | Right-click the chapter and choose **Remove from project**. |
| To another vault | Right-click it and choose **Move to vault…**. This appears when you have more than one vault (see [Vaults](./vaults.md)). |

## Tags

A tag groups documents from any folder. A document can have any number of
tags.

- **Add a tag**: open the document's **Details…**, type in **+ Tag** and
  press Return.
- **Remove a tag**: click the **×** on the tag.
- **See a tag's notes**: click the tag in Details, or under **Tags** in the
  Notes sidebar. Poiesis lists every note with that tag.

## Sort and search a list

Every list has the same header:

- Back and forward buttons (`⌘[` and `⌘]`).
- The list's name.
- **+**, which makes a new document or folder in this list.
- **⋮**, which holds the mode's other actions.

You can do these things in a list:

- **Sort.** Pieces and notes sort by **Edited**, **Created** or **Title**.
  The button shows the current sort.
- **Search.** Type in the field under the header. The list is filtered by
  title, first lines and tags as you type. Inside a folder, the search also
  covers the folders inside it. `Esc` clears the search.
- **Go to the next or previous document.** `⌥⌘←` and `⌥⌘→` open the document
  above or below the open one.
- **Open beside.** `⌥`-click a row to open it next to the document you are
  reading (see [Side by side](./side-by-side.md)).

## Details

A document's **Details…** page holds everything about the document except
its text. There are three ways to open it:

<img src="/img/app/details-light.png" alt="The Details page of a piece: status, description, word goal, tags, folder and links" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<img src="/img/app/details-dark.png" alt="The Details page of a piece: status, description, word goal, tags, folder and links" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

- Click the ⓘ at the top of the page.
- Click the document's **⋮**, then **Details…**.
- Right-click the document's row in a list, then **Details…**.

**Back to text** returns you to the page.

The rows you see depend on the kind of document:

| Row | Shown for |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | Pieces: **draft**, **published** or **archived**. Chapters: **Todo**, **Draft**, **Revised** or **Final**. |
| **Synopsis** or **Description** | A chapter has a synopsis. Every other document has a description. |
| **Word goal** | Pieces and chapters, with a bar that compares the goal with the word count. |
| **Tags** | Every document. |
| **Colour** | Chapters: **Grey**, **Red**, **Amber**, **Green**, **Blue** or **Violet**. |
| **Starred** | Every document. |
| **In** | A chapter: its project, part and number, **Leave out of the numbering**, and a link that opens the project. A piece or a note: its folder, and the choice to move it to the other mode. Any board card that links to the document is also listed here. |
| **Linked to** | Pieces and notes: the documents, projects and characters this document is about (see [Research](./research.md)). |

The bottom of the page shows when the document was created, when it was last
edited, and how many versions it has.

## A document's menu

Right-click a document in any list, or click its **⋮**. The menu has:

- **Details…**, **Open beside**, and **Star** or **Unstar**.
- **Outline**, **Wiki links**, **Notes** and **Version history**. Each opens
  that part of the [Info panel](./finding-your-way.md#the-info-panel).
- The moves listed in [Move a document](#move-a-document).
- **Mark as morning pages** (see
  [Journal & morning pages](./journal-and-morning-pages.md)).
- **Rename…** and **Copy as Markdown**.
- A submenu with every export format (see [Exporting](./exporting.md)).
- **Delete**, which moves the document to the trash.

## Trash

A deleted item is not removed at once. It goes to the **Trash**, at the
bottom of the sidebar. The trash holds deleted documents, projects,
characters, author profiles, boards and board cards.

<img src="/img/app/trash-light.png" alt="The Trash with a deleted document selected, and the Restore and Delete forever buttons" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<img src="/img/app/trash-dark.png" alt="The Trash with a deleted document selected, and the Restore and Delete forever buttons" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

To get something back, or to remove it for good:

1. Click **Trash**.
2. Click an item to see it. A document opens read-only.
3. Press **Restore** to put it back. Or press **Delete forever** to remove it
   now.

**Empty**, at the top of the trash list, removes everything, after asking
you. Items in the trash are permanently deleted after 30 days.

:::note

`⌘⌫` moves the open document to the trash, after asking you.

:::
