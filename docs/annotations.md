# Annotations & margin notes

φ Poiesis gives you two ways to write notes to yourself about a document:

- An **annotation** is about one passage. You can highlight the passage,
  write a comment on it, or both.
- A **margin note** is about the whole document. For example, a reminder of
  what the chapter still needs.

Both are saved with the document.

## Annotate a passage

1. Select the text. A toolbar appears.
2. Click the colour swatch (**Highlight & comment**) and choose a colour. Or
   click **Comment (no highlight)** to leave the text without a colour.
3. The [Info panel](./finding-your-way#the-info-panel) opens on its **Notes**
   tab, with the new annotation ready. Write your comment in its box.

There are five colours: **Yellow**, **Green**, **Blue**, **Purple** and
**Orange**. They are soft, so the text is easy to read in light and dark
themes. **Custom colour** opens the system colour picker, where you can
choose any other colour.

To remove a highlight:

1. Select the text.
2. Open the swatch's menu again.
3. Choose **Remove highlight**.

## Find your notes

All annotations and margin notes for a document are on the Info panel's
**Notes** tab. There are three ways to open it:

- Press `⇧⌘A`.
- Click the document's **⋮**, then **Notes**.
- Right-click in the text and choose **Annotations**.

The tab has two parts: **Margin notes** at the top, then **Annotations**.

## Work with an annotation

Each annotation is a card. The card shows the passage, its colour and your
comment.

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Go to the passage | Click the quoted passage. Poiesis scrolls to it and selects it. |
| Write or change the comment | Type in the card's box (**Add a note…**). |
| Change its colour | Choose another colour or **Custom colour**. Choose **No highlight (comment)** to keep only the comment. |
| Mark it as finished | Click the check (**Mark resolved**). A resolved annotation is dimmed but kept. **Mark unresolved** makes it open again. |
| Remove it | Click the bin (**Delete annotation**). |

When a document has annotations, two more tools appear:

- **Search annotations…** finds annotations by the passage or by your
  comment.
- The menu beside it filters the list: **All**, **Highlights**, **Comments**,
  **Open** or **Resolved**.

### When the text is gone

If you delete or rewrite the passage of an annotation, the annotation is
kept. It is marked **Detached from text**.

To attach it to a new passage:

1. Select the new passage.
2. Click **Reattach to selection**.

## Write a margin note

1. Open the **Notes** tab (`⇧⌘A`).
2. Click **+** beside **Margin notes** (**Add a margin note**).
3. Write in the box (**Write a note about this document…**).

Each margin note shows when you wrote it. Its bin icon (**Delete margin
note**) removes it.

:::note Where they're kept

Annotations and margin notes are saved in the document's own file. When you
copy or back up the file, they are copied with it. Nothing leaves your
computer.

:::
