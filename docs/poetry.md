# Poetry & verse

A poem's lines are the poem, so φ keeps them exactly as you write them.
**Verse** sets your lines at the text's own margin, with your line breaks,
your stanzas and your indents, and every export prints them the same way.

## Write a poem

1. Open a poem in a [project](./collections), or start one with **New
   poem** from the **+** above the list.
2. Type `/verse` and press `Enter`, or press `⌥⌘V`.
3. Write. `Enter` starts a new line within the verse.
4. Press `Enter` twice for a blank line between stanzas.
5. Press `⌘↩` to step out of the verse into an ordinary paragraph below.

Verse, epigraphs, pull quotes and scene breaks are offered in documents
that belong to a project.

## How verse is set

Verse isn't a box. It sits at the same margin as the rest of the text, with
no rule beside it and no indent, and each line stays where you broke it.

- **Your indents are kept.** Start a line with spaces or `Tab` and it keeps
  that indent, on the page and in every export.
- **Nothing is justified or hyphenated**, so no line is stretched or split.
- **A line too long for the printed page** carries on below, indented, the
  way publishers set verse.
- **A blank line** separates stanzas.

Italic and the other formatting work inside verse as anywhere else.

## Turn prose into verse, and back

Already wrote the poem as paragraphs? Select them and choose **Verse**
(`/verse` or `⌥⌘V`). Each paragraph becomes a line, and an empty paragraph
becomes a stanza break. Do it again inside the verse and each line turns
back into a paragraph of its own.

## Open with an epigraph

Type `/epigraph` for an opening quotation. Write the quotation, then its
source in the attribution field below it; `↓` from the quotation's last
line takes you there.

An epigraph for the whole book belongs in the project's
[Book details](./book-details), which sets it on a page of its own.

## Set a line apart

- **Pull-quote** (`/pull-quote`): a line set large, for one statement you
  want the reader to stop on.
- **Scene break** (`/scene`): a centred ornament between sections:
  **Asterism** ⁂, **Stars** \* \* \*, **Fleuron** ❧ or **Blank space**.
  Point at it to switch. In an exported book, every break takes the
  design's ornament, which you can change under **Adjust the design →
  Between scenes**.

| Block | Insert with |
| --- | --- |
| **Verse** | `/verse` or `⌥⌘V` |
| **Epigraph** | `/epigraph` |
| **Pull-quote** | `/pull-quote` |
| **Scene break** | `/scene` |

## Make a collection of poems

Give a project the type **Poetry** and it's made of **Poems**, grouped into
**Parts**. Set it under **Settings → Type** on the project's page, or
choose **Poetry** when Write's Home offers **Start a project**. Everything
else works as for any project: arrange the poems on **Contents**, put a
dedication in the front matter, set a goal.

## Export with the Poetry design

On the project's **Export** page, under **Print book** or **Ebook**, choose
the **Poetry** design. It sets verse as verse: lines kept, nothing
justified or hyphenated, no first-line indents, each poem beginning a new
page with space around it.

:::tip A size for poems
**Digest — 5.5 × 8.5 in** is a usual trim size for a printed collection of
poetry.
:::
