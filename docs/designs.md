# Designs & adjusting them

A **design** decides how your whole book looks, in the way publishers make
their books. It sets:

- the typefaces;
- how chapters open;
- the running heads (the line at the top of each page) and the page numbers;
- the ornament between scenes.

You choose one design, and both the printed book and the ebook use it. If you
want to change a few things, use **Design the book…**. You keep the design
and change only those things, while you look at the pages.

## Choose a design

1. Open the project's **Export** page (see
   [How exporting works](./exporting.md)).
2. Choose **Print book** or **Ebook**.
3. Choose the **Design**.
4. Press **Preview** to see the design on every page.

| Design | The page |
| --- | --- |
| **Classic** | The usual page for published fiction. Garamond typeface. Each chapter opens with a drop cap (a large first letter) and small capitals. The running heads show the author and title. A book with no design chosen uses Classic. |
| **Modern** | A contemporary page. Source Serif typeface. A plain number above each chapter title. Spaced capitals in the running heads. |
| **Literary** | A page with a lot of space. Lora typeface with wide line spacing. Chapters start low on the page. The title is in small capitals. A fleuron (a small leaf ornament) between scenes. |
| **Poetry** | For a collection of poems. Each poem keeps its lines and indentation, with space around it. |

Your own styles are also listed under the designs. See
[Custom styles](./custom-styles.md).

## Design the book

On the **Print book** and **Ebook** tabs, below the design, is the book's
opening page. Beside it is **Design the book…**, with how many changes and
pictures of yours the book has, or *As Classic sets it* (with your design's
name) when it has none.

<img src="/img/app/design-book-dark.png" alt="Design the book: a chapter opening shown large as it will print, with the design's choices beside it as pictures: designs, typefaces, title size and placement, colours" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

1. Click **Design the book…**. The book's pages open large, as they will
   print.
2. Above the pages, choose which **Pages to show**: **Title page**,
   **Chapter opening**, **Text pages**, or, in a book with parts, **Part
   page**.
3. Point at anything on a page: a chapter title, its number, the first
   lines, a scene break, the running head, the page number or the margins.
   φ Poiesis names it.
4. Click it. Its setting opens beside the pages.
5. Make the change. The pages show it a moment later.
6. Press **Done**.

**Undo** and **Redo** (`⌘Z` and `⇧⌘Z`) take a change back or bring it
again. **Start over from Classic** (with your design's name) removes every
change. You can also change the design itself at the top of the settings:
each design is shown as a small page of your own book.

The settings show each choice as what it looks like: typefaces as letters set
in them, placements, first lines, margins and page numbers as small drawings,
colours as swatches, and sizes as a slider. A choice you have not changed is
**As designed**.

| Group | Choice | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Titles** | **Title typeface** | The typeface of the titles, or **Same as the text**. |
| | **Title size** | How large the titles are, from 60% to 180%. |
| | **Titles sit** | **Centred** or **On the left**. |
| | **Title colour** | A swatch, or **Pick a colour**. With black ink, a colour prints as a grey of the same lightness. **Print in colour** changes the ink. |
| | **Chapter numbers** | **Chapter One**, **Chapter 1**, **One**, **1**, **I**, or **None**. |
| **Chapter openings** | **The first lines** | **Drop cap**, **Small capitals** or **Plain**. |
| | **Title placement** | **Near the top**, **A third down** or **Halfway down** the page. |
| | **Chapters start on** | **A right-hand page**, as in most bound books, or **The next page**, with no blank pages (printed book). |
| **Text** | **Typeface** | The typeface of the text. |
| | **Text size** | From 9 to 13 pt. |
| | **Line spacing** | **Tight**, **Standard** or **Airy**. |
| | **Paragraphs** | **Indented** or **Spaced**. |
| | **Alignment** | **Justified** or **Ragged right**. |
| **Scene breaks** | **Between scenes** | The ornament at a scene break: ⁂, \* \* \*, ❧, or **A blank line**. **Your own** puts a small picture of yours there. |
| **Pages** | **Margins** | **Roomy**, **Standard** or **Compact (fewer pages)**. |
| | **Running heads** | On or off: the author and title at the top of the pages (printed book). |
| | **Page numbers** | On or off (printed book). |

Changes belong to the project. To undo one change, set it back to **As
designed**.

## Your pictures

**Your pictures**, in the settings of **Design the book…**, puts your own art
in the book.

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **At every chapter** | A flourish **Above the title** or **Below the title**, a **Band across the top** of the opening page, or a picture **Behind the page**. **A different picture for some chapters** lets you choose another one for any chapter. |
| **At the end of a chapter** | A small closing mark after the last line. |
| **On the title page** | A picture above the title, a band, or a picture behind the page. |
| **On part pages** | A picture above or below the part's title, or behind the page. |
| **Beside the page number** | A tiny mark on every page, with the page number or at the top. |

Scene breaks can also use your own picture: choose **Your own** under
**Scene breaks**.

1. Drop a picture on its place, or click it and choose one. φ Poiesis takes PNG,
   JPEG, SVG and WebP. It keeps a copy in the vault's `assets/` folder.
2. Set its size with the slider, as a part of the line's width. **Back to the
   standard size** returns to the usual size.
3. To change a picture, choose **Replace**. To take it out, choose
   **Remove**.

The places marked **Creative**, a band and a picture behind the page, suit
picture books, fantasy and gift editions. Most novels keep chapter art small.

The print PDF, the ebook and the Word file all carry your pictures. Ebooks and
Word files reflow, so they leave out a picture behind the page and turn a band
into a full-width picture.

## Options

**Options**, below **Design the book…**, holds the rest:

| Choice | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Contents** | How many levels the contents list shows, or no contents list (**None**). |
| **Dedication & epigraph** | Whether these pages show their title and appear in the contents. |
| **Their place on the page** | Where the dedication and epigraph sit: **Near the top**, **A third of the way down**, or **Centred**. |

**Edit styles…** is also here. See [Custom styles](./custom-styles.md).

## Every format follows it

The design is not only for the print PDF. The ebook, the Word file and the web
page also use it. In all of them, chapters open in the same way:

- the chapter number and title together;
- the same drop cap or small capitals;
- the same ornament;
- the same typefaces.

The manuscript for an agent is the one exception. It stays plain, because a
manuscript must be plain.
