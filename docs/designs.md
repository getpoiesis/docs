# Designs & adjusting them

A **design** decides how your whole book looks, in the way publishers make
their books. It sets:

- the typefaces;
- how chapters open;
- the running heads (the line at the top of each page) and the page numbers;
- the ornament between scenes.

You choose one design, and both the printed book and the ebook use it. If you
want to change a few things, use **Adjust the design**. You keep the design
and change only those things.

## Choose a design

1. Open the project's **Export** page (see
   [How exporting works](./exporting)).
2. Choose **Print book** or **Ebook**.
3. Choose the **Design**.
4. Press **Preview** to see the design on every page.

| Design | The page |
| --- | --- |
| **Classic** | The usual page for published fiction. Garamond typeface. Each chapter opens with a drop cap (a large first letter) and small capitals. The running heads show the author and title. A book with no design chosen uses Classic. |
| **Modern** | A contemporary page. Source Serif typeface. A plain number above each chapter title. Spaced capitals in the running heads. |
| **Literary** | A page with a lot of space. Lora typeface with wide line spacing. Chapters start low on the page. The title is in small capitals. A fleuron (a small leaf ornament) between scenes. |
| **Poetry** | For a collection of poems. Each poem keeps its lines and indentation, with space around it. |

Your own styles are also listed under the designs. See
[Custom styles](./custom-styles).

## Adjust the design

1. On the Export page, find **Adjust the design**, below the design.
2. Click it to open the choices.
3. Change the choices you want.

Every choice starts at **As the design**. This means the design decides. When
the row is closed, it shows how many choices you have changed.

| Choice | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Typeface** | The typeface of the text: EB Garamond, Lora, Source Serif, Playfair Display, Inter or Source Sans. |
| **Text size** | From 9.5 to 13 pt (printed book). |
| **Chapter openings** | **Drop cap, 2 lines**; **First words in small capitals**; or **Plain**. |
| **Between scenes** | The ornament at a scene break: ⁂, \* \* \*, ❧, or **A blank line**. |
| **Chapters start on** | **A right-hand page**, as in most bound books, or **The next page**, with no blank pages (printed book). |
| **Running heads** | The author and title at the top of the pages, or none (printed book). |
| **Contents** | How many levels the contents list shows, or no contents list. |
| **Dedication & epigraph** | Whether these pages show their title and appear in the contents. Also where the text sits on the page: near the top, a third of the way down, or centred. |

Adjustments belong to the project. To undo an adjustment, set it to **As the
design** again.

## Every format follows it

The design is not only for the print PDF. The ebook, the Word file and the web
page also use it. In all of them, chapters open in the same way:

- the chapter number and title together;
- the same drop cap or small capitals;
- the same ornament;
- the same typefaces.

The manuscript for an agent is the one exception. It stays plain, because a
manuscript must be plain.
