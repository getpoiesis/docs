# Designs & adjusting them

A **design** sets the whole look of a book the way publishers set theirs:
the typefaces, how chapters open, the running heads and page numbers, the
ornament between scenes. Choose one and the printed book and the ebook both
follow it. **Adjust the design** changes a few things without leaving it.

## Choose a design

1. Open the project's **Export** page, on **Print book** or **Ebook**.
2. Choose the **Design**.
3. Press **Preview** to see it on every page.

| Design | The page |
| --- | --- |
| **Classic** | The trade-fiction page: Garamond, a drop cap and small capitals at each chapter, the author and title in the running heads. A book that hasn't chosen is set in Classic. |
| **Modern** | A contemporary page: Source Serif, a plain numeral over each chapter title, spaced capitals in the running heads. |
| **Literary** | An airy page: Lora with generous leading, deep chapter openings, the title in small capitals, a fleuron between scenes. |
| **Poetry** | For a collection of poems: each poem keeps its lines and indentation, with room around it. |

Your own styles are listed under the designs too ([Custom styles](./custom-styles)).

## Adjust the design

**Adjust the design**, below the design on the Export page, opens these
choices. Each starts at **As the design**, which keeps the design's own
choice; the folded row says how many you've changed.

| Choice | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| **Typeface** | The text face: EB Garamond, Lora, Source Serif, Playfair Display, Inter or Source Sans. |
| **Text size** | From 9.5 to 13 pt (printed book). |
| **Chapter openings** | **Drop cap, 2 lines**; **First words in small capitals**; or **Plain**. |
| **Between scenes** | The ornament at a scene break: ⁂, \* \* \*, ❧, or **A blank line**. |
| **Chapters start on** | **A right-hand page**, as bound books do, or **The next page**, with no blank pages (printed book). |
| **Running heads** | The author and title at the top of the pages, or none (printed book). |
| **Contents** | How deep the contents list goes, or none. |
| **Dedication & epigraph** | Whether those pages show their title and appear in the contents, and where they sit on their page: near the top, a third of the way down, or centred. |

Adjustments belong to the project, and **As the design** puts any of them
back.

## Every format follows it

The design isn't only for the PDF. The ebook, the Word file and the web page
open chapters the same way: the chapter number and title together, the same
drop cap or small capitals, the same ornament, the same typefaces. Only the
manuscript for an agent stays plain, because a manuscript is meant to be.
