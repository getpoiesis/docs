# Focus & Sanctuary

When it's time to write, the rest of φ should step aside. **Sanctuary**
clears away everything but the page, and a few quieter settings keep your
eyes on the line you're writing.

## Go into Sanctuary

1. Open the document you want to write.
2. Press `⌘.`, or click **Sanctuary** at the top right of the page.
3. Write.
4. Press `Esc` or `⌘.` to come back.

Sanctuary is also in the **View** menu, the document's ⋮ menu and the
command palette (`⌘P`). It's there for a document and for the
[graph](./links-and-graph).

## While you're in Sanctuary

The sidebar, the list, the Info panel and the buttons go away. What's left:

- **The sentence you're in stays at full strength**, and the rest of the
  text is dimmed. If **Focus typing** is set to **Paragraph**, the whole
  paragraph stays lit. To keep everything at full strength, turn off
  **Settings → Appearance → Sanctuary dims the rest**.
- **A quiet line at the top says where the document lives**: its mode, its
  project, part and chapter, or its folder. Click a step to go there; that
  leaves Sanctuary with the document still open.
- **The word count moves to the bottom centre**, against the goal when
  there is one.
- **Typewriter scrolling** has its own button at the top right.

The ☰ at the top left (**Show writing tools**) brings everything back.
Sanctuary stays in the window rather than going full screen; use the
**View** menu's full-screen item if you want both. Opening a page that
isn't a document, such as the calendar or a board, ends it by itself.

With documents [side by side](./side-by-side), Sanctuary keeps the panes,
and the dimming and typewriter scrolling apply only to the pane you're
writing in.

## Keep your line in the middle

**Typewriter scrolling** holds the line you're writing in the middle of the
window, so your eyes stay put and the text rises to meet them. Turn it on
or off with `⇧⌘T`, **View → Typewriter Scrolling**, the document's ⋮ menu,
the button in Sanctuary, or **Settings → Editor → Typewriter scrolling**.

## Dim what you're not writing

**Focus typing** dims everything but where you are, in or out of
Sanctuary:

| Setting | What stays lit |
| --- | --- |
| **Sentence** | The sentence you're in. |
| **Paragraph** | The paragraph you're in. |
| **Off** | Everything. (Sanctuary still dims to the sentence, unless you've turned that off.) |

Set it in **View → Focus Typing**, in **Settings → Editor → Focus typing**,
or step through the three with **Cycle focus typing** in the command
palette.

## Read without editing

**Read mode** shows the document read-only, so you can go through a draft
without stray keystrokes. Toggle it with `⌘E`, **View → Reading Mode**,
**Read mode** in the document's ⋮ menu, or **Toggle reading mode** in the
command palette.

## Your writing sessions

A writing session, or sitting, is one stretch of work. It starts with your
first keystroke and counts the words you add; deleting doesn't subtract, so
a sitting spent revising still shows the work you did.

A sitting counts only the time you're writing. It pauses after a minute
without typing, when you switch to another app, when you leave the page,
and in read mode; the next keystroke picks it up. After twenty minutes
without a word, it ends.

- **Write**'s Home shows the minutes of the current sitting on its **Today**
  card.
- The document's statistics (click the word count, then click it again)
  keep your **Longest session** and **Best session words**.

To decide when a sitting starts yourself, turn off **Start sessions
automatically** in **Settings → Setup**, and use **Start writing session**
and **End writing session** in the command palette.
