# Notes & capture

**Notes** is the mode for everything around your writing: an idea, a source,
a line you overheard, a thought about chapter four. A note has no status and
no word goal. You type a thought, press Return, and go back to your work.

## Capture a thought

1. Choose **Notes** in the mode switch at the top of the sidebar (`⌘2`).
   Notes opens on its **Home** page.
2. Click **Capture a thought…** and type.
3. To tag the note, add a `#word`. To link it to a document, type `[[` and
   the document's title.
4. Press Return. The note is saved, and the box is empty for the next one.

The note does not open. You stay where you were. The new note appears at the
top of **Recently edited** and in **All notes**.

- A short thought becomes the note's title.
- A long thought is shortened to make the title. The full thought is kept as
  the note's text.
- Only the first `#word` becomes a tag. The word also stays in the text as
  you wrote it.

## Capture from anywhere

You don't need to be in φ Poiesis to catch a thought. Press `⌥⇧⌘N`
(**Ctrl + Alt + Shift + N** on Windows and Linux) anywhere, even with φ in
the background. A small window opens.

1. Type the thought. The first line becomes the note's title.
2. Press `⌘↵` (or **Save**). The note goes to the open vault's **Inbox**.

Press `Esc` to put the window away. What you typed waits there for next
time. You can also open the window from the φ in the menu bar, or with
**File → Quick Capture**.

To change the shortcut, or to turn it off, go to **Settings** → **Shortcuts**
→ **Quick capture**. Click the key and press the one you want. If another
app already uses it, φ says so; pick another.

### The Inbox

**Inbox** is at the top of Notes' sidebar, under **Home**. It holds what you
captured that you haven't dealt with yet, and its count always shows.
`⌘G` then `I` opens it from anywhere.

When you have dealt with a capture, right-click it and choose **Done with it
(out of the Inbox)**. It leaves the Inbox and stays in your notes.

## Capture from a list

The same box is at the top of the list when you look at **All notes**, a
folder or a tag. Where the note goes depends on the list you are looking at:

| You're looking at | The note goes |
| --- | --- |
| **All notes** | In no folder, tagged with its first `#word`. |
| A folder | In that folder, tagged with its first `#word`. |
| A tag | Under that tag, whatever you typed. |

To write a longer note, press **+** at the top of the list (**New note**), or
press `⌘N` while you are in Notes. If you are in a folder, the new note goes
in that folder, however you make it: **+**, `⌘N`, the list's **⋮** or `⌘K`.
In a tag's list, the new note gets that tag.

## Notes' Home

Notes opens on Home. On the left you see:

- **Capture a thought…**: the quickest way to make a note.
- **Recently edited**: the last few notes you changed. Each shows how long
  ago you changed it and its first line. **All notes** opens the full list.
- **Open action items**: checklist items from your notes that are on a board
  and are not done yet. This section appears only when you have some. See
  [Boards](./boards.md).

On the right you see:

- The month. Darker days are days when you edited more notes. **Calendar**
  opens the calendar.
- **Memories**, when you have something from this day in an earlier year (see
  [Memories & celebrations](./memories-and-celebrations.md)).
- Your **Starred** notes.
- The **Tags** you use most. Click a tag to list its notes.

## Find a note again

In Notes, the sidebar lists these places:

| Place | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **Inbox** | What you sent with quick capture and haven't dealt with yet. |
| **All notes** | Every note that belongs to no project. |
| **Starred** | The notes you starred. |
| **Folders** | Your folders, each with the number of notes in it. **+** beside the heading makes a new folder. |
| **Tags** | Every tag on your notes, each with the number of notes. |

Each list has a search field at the top. It filters the list by title, first
lines and tags. Each list also has a sort: **Edited**, **Created** or
**Title**.

**Find unlinked notes.** Open **All notes**, then open the list's sort menu
and choose **Only unlinked notes**. The list shows only the notes with no
links to them and no links from them: the ones you have not connected to
anything yet. Choose it again to see every note.

`⌘K` finds any document by name, from anywhere in φ Poiesis. See
[Search & replace](./search-and-replace.md).

## Star a note

Star the notes you open most often. There are three ways:

- Click the **⋮** at the top of the open note, then **Star**.
- Right-click the note in a list (or click its **⋮**), then **Star**.
- Click the star on the note's **Details…** page.

Do the same again to remove the star.

Starred notes are listed under **Starred** in the sidebar and on Home. They
also show a small star in every list.

A note in a folder shows the folder's name on its row. This happens in **All
notes** and in every other list except the folder's own list.

## Tag a note

A tag groups notes from any folder. A note can have as many tags as you like.

- **Add a tag**: open the note's **Details…**, type in **+ Tag** and press
  Return. A `#word` in a captured thought also adds a tag.
- **Remove a tag**: click the **×** on the tag in Details.
- **See a tag's notes**: click the tag under **Tags** in the sidebar, on
  Home, or in Details.

In a list, each note shows its first tag under its first line.

:::tip Notes and pieces are one kind of document

A piece is a piece of writing in Write that is outside any project. A note
can become a piece, and a piece can become a note.

- To turn a note into a piece, right-click it (or use its **⋮**) and choose
  **Move to Write's pieces**.
- To turn a piece into a note, choose **Move to Notes**.

See [Organizing](./organizing.md).

:::
