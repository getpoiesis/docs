# Notes & capture

**Notes** is the mode for everything that isn't the writing itself: an idea,
a source, a line you overheard, a thought about chapter four. A note has no
status, no word goal and no count to watch, so it costs nothing to make one.
You type a thought, press Return, and get back to work.

## Capture a thought

1. Choose **Notes** in the mode switch at the top of the sidebar (`⌘2`). You
   land on Notes' **Home**.
2. Click **Capture a thought…** and type.
3. Add a `#word` to tag it, or `[[` and a title to link it to a document.
4. Press Return. The note is saved and the box is empty for the next one.

The note isn't opened; you stay where you were. It shows at the top of
**Recently edited**, and in **All notes**.

A short thought becomes the note's title. A long one is cut to a title, and
the whole of it is kept as the note's text. Only the first `#word` becomes a
tag; the word stays in the text as you wrote it.

## Capture from a list

The same box sits at the top of the list when you're looking at **All notes**,
a folder or a tag. A note captured there lands where you're looking:

| You're looking at | The note goes |
| --- | --- |
| **All notes** | In no folder, tagged with its first `#word`. |
| A folder | In that folder, tagged with its first `#word`. |
| A tag | Under that tag, whatever you typed. |

To write something longer, press **+** at the top of the list (**New note**),
or `⌘N` while you're in Notes. A note made with **+** while you're in a folder
goes in that folder.

## Notes' Home

Home is where Notes opens. On the left:

- **Capture a thought…**, the quickest way in.
- **Recently edited**: the last few notes you touched, with how long ago and
  their opening line. **All notes** opens the full list.
- **Open action items**: checklist items from your notes that are on a board
  and not yet done. It's there only when you have some. See
  [Boards](./boards).

On the right, the month (darker days are days you edited more notes, and
**Calendar** opens the calendar), your **Starred** notes, and your most used
**Tags**. Click a tag to list its notes.

## Find a note again

The sidebar in Notes lists the ways in:

| Place | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **All notes** | Every note that belongs to no project. |
| **Starred** | The notes you starred. |
| **Unlinked** | Notes that nothing links to and that link to nothing. Good for finding loose threads. |
| **Folders** | Your folders, each with how many notes it holds. **+** beside the heading makes a new one. |
| **Tags** | Every tag on your notes, with a count. |

Each list has a search field at the top that narrows it by title, opening
text and tags, and a sort: **Last edited**, **Date created** or **Title**.
`⌘K` finds any document by name from anywhere. See
[Search & replace](./search-and-replace).

## Star a note

Star what you reach for most. Any of these works, and choosing it again
unstars it:

- The **⋮** at the top of the open note → **Star**.
- Right-click the note in a list, or its **⋮** → **Star**.
- The star on the note's **Details…** page.

Starred notes are listed under **Starred** in the sidebar and on Home.

## Tag a note

A tag cuts across folders, and a note can carry as many as you like.

- **Add one**: open the note's **Details…**, type in **+ Tag** and press
  Return. Capturing with a `#word` adds one too.
- **Remove one**: click the **×** on it in Details.
- **Browse one**: click it under **Tags** in the sidebar, on Home, or in
  Details.

A list shows each note's first tag under its opening line.

:::tip Notes and pieces are one kind of document

A note can become a piece of writing, and back. Right-click it (or use its
**⋮**) and choose **Move to Write's pieces**. A piece moves the other way
with **Move to Notes**. See [Organizing](./organizing).

:::
