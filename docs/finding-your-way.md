# A tour of the window

φ's window has three columns: the **sidebar**, the **list** beside it, and the
**page** you write on, with an **Info** panel you can open on the right. Each
mode, Write, Notes and Journal, opens on a **Home** of its own. Once you know
these four parts, you know where everything is.

## Get around

1. Pick a mode at the top of the sidebar: **Write**, **Notes** or
   **Journal**.
2. Pick a place in the sidebar, such as a project or **All notes**. The list
   beside it shows what's there.
3. Pick a row in the list. It opens on the page, and the list stays put.
4. Press `⇧⌘I` to open the **Info** panel beside the page.
5. Press `⌘[` to go back to where you were.

## The sidebar

From top to bottom:

- **The vault's name.** Click it for your vaults, **Open another vault…**,
  **New vault…**, **Show in Finder** (**Show in File Explorer** on Windows) and **Import…**. With more than one
  vault, it also offers **Merge into another vault…**. See [Vaults](./vaults).
- **The sliders** beside it open **Settings** (`⌘,`).
- **Write · Notes · Journal**, the mode switch. `⌘1`, `⌘2` and `⌘3` take you
  to each mode's Home. A vault that uses only one mode has no switch.
- **The mode's places**, in groups (below).
- **At the foot:** what the vault is doing (**Saving…**, **Indexing…**), the
  **Trash**, and the **Alpha** badge, which is also how to send feedback.

| Mode | What the sidebar lists |
| --- | --- |
| **Write** | **Home**, **Pieces**, **Projects** (each project, and **New project**), **World** (**Characters**, **Authors**, **Research**), **Places** (**Boards**, **Graph**, **Calendar**) |
| **Notes** | **Home**, **All notes**, **Starred**, **Unlinked**, **Folders** (with a **+** for a new folder), **Tags**, **Places** (**Boards**, **Graph**, **Calendar**, **Templates**) |
| **Journal** | **Home**, **Today**, **All entries**, **Morning pages**, **Sealed**, **Places** (**Calendar**) |

Click a project and its own page opens, with its outline in the list. While
you're in it, its parts sit under it in the sidebar: **Contents**,
**Research**, **Board**, **Read** and **Export**.

Right-click a project or a folder for its menu. Drag a note onto a folder in
the sidebar to file it there. Which places each mode shows is yours to change
in [Setting up](./setup).

## The list

The second column lists whatever you picked: a project's chapters, your
pieces, a folder of notes, the journal's days, the boards, the trash.

Along its top:

- **‹ ›**: back and forward, with where you are beside them (**Write ·
  Projects**, say).
- **The title** of what's listed.
- **The sort** on pieces and notes: **Edited**, **Created** or **Title**.
- **+**: make a new one here. In a project it offers a new chapter (`⌘N`) or
  a new part (`⇧⌘N`), named for the kind of project.
- **⋮**: what you need less often. In a project, it also holds the project's
  pages and settings: **Contents**, **Icon…**, **Colour…**, **Overview**,
  **Research**, **Read**, **Open project board**, **Export manuscript…** and
  **Delete project…**.

Pieces, notes, characters, authors and research have a search field under
the title that narrows the list as you type; `Esc` clears it.

**All notes**, a folder and a tag also carry a **Capture a thought…** box.
Type a line and press `Enter`, and it's saved as a note, in the folder or
under the tag you're looking at. In **All notes** or a folder, a `#word` in
the line tags it.

**Folders open in place.** Open a folder in Pieces or in Notes and the list
shows what's inside, with the path above it (**All › Essays › Drafts**).
Click a step of the path to go back out. Drop a document on a folder, or on a
step of the path, to move it there.

## The page

The page carries the title and the words. With a document open, the buttons
at the top right are:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Split view** (`⌘\`) | Opens a second pane beside this one. See [Documents side by side](./side-by-side). |
| **⋮** | The document's menu: star, add to a board, read mode, typewriter scrolling, check spelling, save a version, export, move to the trash and more. |
| **Details…** (the ⓘ) | The document's status, synopsis or description, word goal, tags, colour and where it lives. See [Organizing your work](./organizing). |
| **Info** (`⇧⌘I`) | Opens or closes the Info panel. |
| **Sanctuary** (`⌘.`) | Hides everything but the page. See [Sanctuary & writing sessions](./focus-and-writing-modes). |

The word count sits in the bottom-right corner, or your words against the
goal if the document has one. Click it to open **Info** on its **Outline**;
click it again for the full **Document stats**.

## The Info panel

<img src="/img/app/outline-light.png" alt="The Info panel beside a research page, on its Outline tab: words, reading time, headings and footnotes" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<img src="/img/app/outline-dark.png" alt="The Info panel beside a research page, on its Outline tab: words, reading time, headings and footnotes" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

**Info** (`⇧⌘I`) opens a panel on the right of a document, in five tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Outline** | Words, reading time and the goal; the headings and footnotes; **Add to board…** and **Save version…**. |
| **Links** | What this document links to and what links to it, the notes linked to it, and **Local graph**. See [Links & the graph](./links-and-graph). |
| **Notes** | Its highlights, comments and margin notes (`⇧⌘A`). See [Annotations](./annotations). |
| **Tasks** | Its checklist items, and the board cards tracking them. |
| **History** | Its versions. See [Versions & backup](./versions-and-backup). |

The [dictionary](./dictionary) joins them as a sixth tab while you use it
(`⇧⌘D`). A morning page shows **The practice** and **History** instead, and a
project's own pages show the **Project**.

The panel stays open until you close it. Once closed, it stays closed for
every document, even after you quit, until you open it again. When the window
is too narrow for everything, it steps aside.

Drag the thin divider between two columns to resize them, or focus it and use
the arrow keys. φ remembers the widths for each vault. Double-click the Info
panel's divider, or drag it most of the way closed, to close the panel.

## Each mode's Home

`⌘⇧H`, or **Home** at the top of the sidebar, takes you to the Home of the
mode you're in.

- **Write** greets you with the chapter to **Continue**, the projects you're
  **Working on**, recent **Pieces** and **Recent research**. Beside them:
  **Today** (words today, your streak, this sitting, days this week), the
  month, and what's **Due this week** on your boards.
- **Notes** opens on **Capture a thought…**, with what you **Recently
  edited** and your **Open action items**. Beside them: the month, your
  **Starred** notes and your **Tags**.
- **Journal** opens on today's entry, **This week** and the days before.
  Beside them: today's **Morning page**, the month, and **On this day**: what
  you wrote on this date in other years.

## Find anything: `⌘K`

`⌘K` (**File → Find a Document…**) finds a document, a project or a
character by name. Before you type, it shows:

- **Open now**: the documents you've opened and not closed. The one on screen
  is marked **here now**.
- **Go to**: Home, the modes and places; more of them as you type.
- **Create**: a new document, and more as you type.

Type and it searches every document by title and by what's in it, across all
three modes, and says which mode each one is in.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Enter` | Opens the highlighted result. |
| `⌥↵` | Opens it beside the document you're in. |
| `⌘↵` | Opens it and keeps the palette open, so you can open several. |
| `⌘W` | Closes the highlighted open document; the palette stays. |
| `Esc` | Closes the palette. |

## Run a command: `⌘P`

`⌘P` (**File → Command Palette…**) holds the commands: new documents,
exports, versions, vaults, typewriter scrolling, checking for updates and the
rest. Before you type, it shows what you've run **Lately**; type to search
every command.

## Go back and forth

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| `⌘[` / `⌘]` | Back and forward through where you've been, in the list and on the page. Your mouse's side buttons do the same. |
| `⌥⌘←` / `⌥⌘→` | The document above or below in the list: the chapter before or after, the next note, the day before. |
| `⌘W` | Closes the document and keeps its list. A document opened from somewhere else (the calendar, a board, the graph) closes back to where you opened it. With no document open, `⌘W` takes you to the mode's Home. |
| `⌘⇧H` | The mode's Home. |
