# A tour of the window

φ's window has three columns, from left to right:

- The **sidebar**, where you choose a mode and a place in it.
- The **list**, which shows what is in that place.
- The **page**, where you write.

You can also open an **Info** panel on the right of the page. It shows
details about the open document.

φ has three modes: **Write**, **Notes** and **Journal**. Each mode has its
own start screen, called **Home**.

## Get around

1. Choose a mode at the top of the sidebar: **Write**, **Notes** or
   **Journal**.
2. Choose a place in the sidebar, such as a project or **All notes**. The
   list shows what is in it.
3. Click a row in the list. The document opens on the page. The list stays
   where it is.
4. Press `⇧⌘I` to open the **Info** panel beside the page.
5. Press `⌘[` to go back to where you were.

## The sidebar

From top to bottom, the sidebar has:

- **The vault's name.** A vault is the folder that holds your writing. Click
  the name to open the vault menu. It lists your vaults, then **Open another
  vault…**, **New vault…**, **Show in Finder** (**Show in File Explorer** on
  Windows) and **Import…**. If you have more than one vault, it also has
  **Merge into another vault…**. See [Vaults](./vaults).
- **The sliders** beside the name. They open **Settings** (`⌘,`).
- **Write · Notes · Journal**, the mode switch. `⌘1`, `⌘2` and `⌘3` open
  the Home of each mode. A vault that uses only one mode has no switch.
- **The mode's places**, in groups. The table below lists them.
- **At the bottom:** what the vault is doing now (**Saving…**,
  **Indexing…**), the **Trash**, and the **Beta** badge. Click the badge to
  send feedback.

| Mode | What the sidebar lists |
| --- | --- |
| **Write** | **Home**, **Pieces**, **Projects** (each project, **New project**, and **Put away** when you've put some away), **World** (**Characters**, **Authors**, **Research**), **Places** (**Graph**, **Calendar**) |
| **Notes** | **Home**, **All notes**, **Starred**, **Unlinked**, **Folders** (with a **+** for a new folder), **Tags**, **Places** (**Boards**, **Graph**, **Calendar**, **Templates**) |
| **Journal** | **Home**, **Today**, **All entries**, **Morning pages**, **Sealed**, **Places** (**Calendar**) |

Click a project to open the project's own page. The list shows its outline.
While the project is open, the sidebar shows these under it: **Contents**,
**Research**, **Characters**, **Board**, **Read** and **Export**.

Right-click a project or a folder to open its menu. To put a note in a
folder, drag the note onto the folder in the sidebar.

You can choose which places each mode shows. See [Setting up](./setup).

## The list

The second column lists what is in the place you chose: a project's
chapters, your pieces, a folder of notes, the journal's days, the boards or
the trash.

At the top of the list:

- **‹ ›** go back and forward. Beside them, φ shows where you are, for
  example **Write · Projects**.
- **The title** names what the list shows.
- **The sort** appears on pieces and notes: **Edited**, **Created** or
  **Title**.
- **+** makes a new document here. In a project, it offers a new chapter
  (`⌘N`) or a new part (`⇧⌘N`). Their names depend on the kind of project.
- **⋮** opens a menu of less common actions. In a project, this menu also
  has the project's pages and settings: **Contents**, **Icon…**,
  **Colour…**, **Overview**, **Research**, **Read**, **Open project
  board**, **Export manuscript…** and **Delete project…**.

**Search the list.** Pieces, notes, characters, authors and research have a
search field under the title. The list gets shorter as you type. Press `Esc`
to clear the search.

**Capture a thought.** **All notes**, a folder and a tag have a **Capture a
thought…** box. Type a line and press `Enter`. φ saves the line as a note,
in the folder or with the tag you are looking at. In **All notes** or a
folder, a `#word` in the line adds that tag to the note.

**Folders open in place.** Open a folder in Pieces or in Notes, and the list
shows what is inside it. The path is above the list, for example **All ›
Essays › Drafts**. Click a step of the path to go back to it. To move a
document, drag it onto a folder or onto a step of the path.

## The page

The page shows the title and the text. When a document is open, these
buttons are at the top right:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Split view** (`⌘\`) | Opens a second pane beside this one. See [Documents side by side](./side-by-side). |
| **⋮** | The document's menu: star, add to a board, read mode, typewriter scrolling, check spelling, save a version, export, move to the trash and more. |
| **Details…** (the ⓘ) | The document's status, synopsis or description, word goal, tags, colour and where it lives. See [Organizing your work](./organizing). |
| **Info** (`⇧⌘I`) | Opens or closes the Info panel. |
| **Sanctuary** (`⌘.`) | Hides everything but the page. See [Sanctuary & writing sessions](./focus-and-writing-modes). |

The word count is in the bottom-right corner. If the document has a word
goal, the corner shows your words and the goal. Click it once to open the
**Outline** tab of **Info**. Click it again to open the full **Document
stats**.

## The Info panel

<img src="/img/app/outline-light.png" alt="The Info panel beside a research page, on its Outline tab: words, reading time, headings and footnotes" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<img src="/img/app/outline-dark.png" alt="The Info panel beside a research page, on its Outline tab: words, reading time, headings and footnotes" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

Press `⇧⌘I`, or click **Info**, to open a panel on the right of a document.
It has five tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Outline** | Words, reading time and the goal; the headings and footnotes; **Add to board…** and **Save version…**. |
| **Links** | What this document links to and what links to it, the notes linked to it, and **Local graph**. See [Links & the graph](./links-and-graph). |
| **Notes** | Its highlights, comments and margin notes (`⇧⌘A`). See [Annotations](./annotations). |
| **Tasks** | Its checklist items, and the board cards tracking them. |
| **History** | Its versions. See [Versions & backup](./versions-and-backup). |

Some documents show different tabs:

- While you use the [dictionary](./dictionary) (`⇧⌘D`), it is a sixth tab.
- A morning page shows **The practice** and **History** instead.
- A project's own pages show the **Project**.

**Open and closed.** The panel stays open until you close it. After you
close it, it stays closed for every document, even after you quit φ, until
you open it again. If the window is too narrow to show everything, φ hides
the panel without closing it. Make the window wider and the panel comes
back.

**Change the widths.** Drag the thin divider between two columns to make
them wider or narrower. You can also move to the divider with the keyboard
and press the arrow keys. φ remembers the widths for each vault.

**Close the panel with its divider.** Double-click the Info panel's divider,
or drag it most of the way closed.

## Each mode's Home

To open the Home of the mode you are in, press `⌘⇧H` or click **Home** at
the top of the sidebar.

**Write** shows:

- The chapter to **Continue**, the projects you are **Working on**, recent
  **Pieces** and **Recent research**.
- Beside them: **Today** (words today, your streak, this sitting, days this
  week), the month, and what is **Due this week** on your boards.

**Notes** shows:

- **Capture a thought…**, the notes you **Recently edited** and your **Open
  action items**.
- Beside them: the month, your **Starred** notes and your **Tags**.

**Journal** shows:

- Today's entry, **This week** and the days before.
- Beside them: today's **Morning page**, the month, and **On this day**.
  **On this day** shows what you wrote on this date in other years.

## Find anything: `⌘K`

Press `⌘K` (**File → Find a Document…**) to find a document, a project or a
character by name. A small window opens, called the palette. Before you
type, it shows:

- **Open now**: the documents you have opened and not closed. The one on
  screen is marked **here now**.
- **Go to**: Home, the modes and the places. More appear as you type.
- **Create**: a new document. More choices appear as you type.

When you type, φ searches every document in all three modes. It searches the
titles and the text. Each result says which mode it is in.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Enter` | Opens the highlighted result. |
| `⌥↵` | Opens it beside the document you're in. |
| `⌘↵` | Opens it and keeps the palette open, so you can open several. |
| `⌘W` | Closes the highlighted open document; the palette stays. |
| `Esc` | Closes the palette. |

## Run a command: `⌘P`

Press `⌘P` (**File → Command Palette…**) to run a command. The commands
include new documents, exports, versions, vaults, typewriter scrolling,
checking for updates and more. Before you type, the palette shows the
commands you ran **Lately**. Type to search every command.

## Go back and forth

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| `⌘[` / `⌘]` | Back and forward through where you've been, in the list and on the page. Your mouse's side buttons do the same. |
| `⌥⌘←` / `⌥⌘→` | The document above or below in the list: the chapter before or after, the next note, the day before. |
| `⌘W` | Closes the document and keeps its list. A document opened from somewhere else (the calendar, a board, the graph) closes back to where you opened it. With no document open, `⌘W` takes you to the mode's Home. |
| `⌘⇧H` | The mode's Home. |
