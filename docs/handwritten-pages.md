# Handwritten pages

With φ on the iPad or an Android tablet (coming soon), you can write a
document by hand with a pen. You can write on lined, dotted, grid or blank
paper.

When you open that document on your computer, you see the pages as you
wrote them, whichever tablet you wrote them on. They stay sharp at any size.

You can write by hand only on a tablet. On the computer, you can read the
pages, edit their text, and go back to earlier versions.

## Read the pages or their text

A handwritten document has two views. Choose one at the top of the page:

- **Ink** shows your handwritten pages on their paper, one below the other.
  Pen and pencil lines keep the thickness you gave them. Highlighter shows
  under the words.
- **Text** shows the words read from your handwriting. You can edit them
  here like any other document, and your changes go to your tablets too.

A handwritten document has one text. Until you edit it, it follows your
handwriting: when a tablet reads new words from the pages, the text updates.
Once you edit it, φ doesn't change it on its own. On the tablet,
**Update from handwriting** brings it back in line with the pages.

While a document is open, φ remembers which view you chose for it.

## Earlier versions

A handwritten document's versions keep its pages as well as its text. To
see one, open the document's history and choose a version:

- **Ink** shows the pages as they were then.
- **Show changes** shows the pages as they are now. Lines you added since
  that version are in green, and lines you removed since show faintly.
- **Restore** brings back the pages and the text together.

Versions saved before φ 0.19 kept only the text. They say so, and restoring
one leaves the pages as they are.

## Colours

Black ink and φ's own slate colour change with your
[colour theme](themes-and-languages). For example, a page written in black
shows in white on a dark theme.

Other colours stay the same colour. On a dark page, φ makes them a little
lighter so that you can still read them.

## A page that can't be read

If a page's file is damaged, for example because it was still syncing when
you opened the document, you see the message *This page couldn't be read*
in its place. Open the document on the tablet it was written on. When it
is saved again, the page shows on the computer too.

## Where the pages live

φ keeps the handwritten pages in your [vault](vaults), with the document. A
vault is the folder where φ keeps your documents. The pages sync together
with the document.

The pages also go with the document when you move it to the trash, restore
it, or move it to another vault.
