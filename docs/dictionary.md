# Dictionary & thesaurus

φ can look a word up for its meaning, or for a better one, beside the page
you're writing. It works the way an e-reader's dictionary does: you install
dictionary packs, and every lookup happens on your computer.

## Look up a word

1. Select the word on the page.
2. In the toolbar above it, click **More tools** (›), then **Look up word**.
3. Read the definition in the **Dictionary** tab of the Info panel.

To look up a word you haven't written yet, press `⌘⇧D` (or choose **View →
Dictionary**, or **Open dictionary** in the document's ⋮ menu or the
command palette), type it in **Look up a word…** and press `Enter`. Press
`⌘⇧D` again to give the panel back to its **Outline**.

Each result shows the definition and, where the pack has them, synonyms,
labelled with the pack it came from. Click any synonym or cross-reference
to look that word up in turn.

You don't need the exact dictionary form: *running*, *ledgers* and *cities*
find *run*, *ledger* and *city*. Many packs carry their own list of forms
too, so a Spanish dictionary finds *correr* from *corriendo*.

The **Dictionary** tab joins the Info panel only while you're using it, and
only when a document is open.

## Install a dictionary

φ doesn't come with a dictionary, so the first time you open the tab it
says **No dictionaries installed.** It reads the widely used **StarDict**
format:

1. Download a pack. Good free sources are
   [freedict.org](https://freedict.org) and
   [wikdict.com](https://www.wikdict.com). For English definitions with
   synonyms, a **WordNet** StarDict pack works well.
2. Unzip it. You'll have a folder with files ending `.ifo`, `.idx` and
   `.dict` (sometimes `.idx.gz` or `.dict.dz`; both are fine).
3. Open **Settings → Language → Dictionary & Thesaurus**, click **Install
   dictionary pack…**, and choose the folder.

The pack is ready at once. Install as many as you like: a lookup searches
them all. Because packs are files you choose, they can be in any language,
or bilingual, to translate as you write.

## Manage your packs

**Settings → Language → Dictionary & Thesaurus** lists each pack with how
many words it holds. To remove one, click the bin beside it and confirm
**Remove dictionary?**

:::note Nothing leaves your computer
Installed packs are kept in φ's own application folder, and no lookup ever
goes online.
:::
