# Dictionary & thesaurus

φ can show you what a word means, or other words with a similar meaning,
next to the page you're writing. First you install one or more dictionary
packs. After that, every lookup happens on your computer.

## Look up a word

To look up a word in your text:

1. Select the word on the page.
2. In the toolbar above it, click **More tools** (›), then **Look up word**.
3. Read the definition in the **Dictionary** tab of the Info panel, the
   panel beside your page.

To look up a word you haven't written:

1. Press `⌘⇧D`. You can also choose **View → Dictionary**, or **Open
   dictionary** in the document's ⋮ menu or in the command palette.
2. Type the word in **Look up a word…** and press `Enter`.
3. Press `⌘⇧D` again to return the panel to its **Outline** tab.

Each result shows the definition and the name of the pack it comes from.
If the pack has synonyms, the result shows them too. Click a synonym or a
cross-reference to look up that word.

You don't need to type the form of the word that a dictionary lists.
*Running*, *ledgers* and *cities* find *run*, *ledger* and *city*. Many
packs also have their own list of word forms, so a Spanish dictionary finds
*correr* from *corriendo*.

The **Dictionary** tab is in the Info panel only while you're using it, and
only when a document is open.

## Install a dictionary

φ doesn't come with a dictionary. The first time you open the tab, it says
**No dictionaries installed.** φ reads dictionary packs in the common
**StarDict** format. To install one:

1. Download a pack. Good free sources are
   [freedict.org](https://freedict.org) and
   [wikdict.com](https://www.wikdict.com). For English definitions with
   synonyms, a **WordNet** StarDict pack works well.
2. Unzip it. You get a folder with files ending in `.ifo`, `.idx` and
   `.dict`. Sometimes they end in `.idx.gz` or `.dict.dz`, which also work.
3. Open **Settings → Language → Dictionary & Thesaurus**.
4. Click **Install dictionary pack…** and choose the folder.

You can use the pack immediately. Install as many packs as you like: a
lookup searches all of them. A pack can be in any language. A bilingual
pack lets you translate as you write.

## Manage your packs

**Settings → Language → Dictionary & Thesaurus** lists each pack and the
number of words in it. To remove a pack, click the bin beside it and
confirm **Remove dictionary?**

:::note Nothing leaves your computer
φ keeps installed packs in its own application folder. A lookup never goes
online.
:::
