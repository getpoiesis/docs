# Memories & celebrations

φ Poiesis remembers your writing for you. It shows you what you wrote on this
day in earlier years, tells the story of each year you wrote, and marks the
milestones you reach with a short burst of confetti.

## Memories

A memory is something from this day in an earlier year: a document, a
journal entry, a book you began, or a milestone you reached, such as a long
streak. Memories come from the whole vault.

**On each Home.** The Home of Write, Notes and Journal shows a **Memories**
card beside the page. It shows one memory at a time, with a line such as
*3 years ago today* and the opening of what you wrote, in the type you write
in. When there is more than one, the dots under it move between them. Click
the memory to open it.

When nothing happened on this exact day, the card shows the same week in
earlier years instead, with a line such as *A year ago this week*.

**Morning pages stay private.** A memory never shows the text of your
morning pages. It only says *You wrote your morning pages.*, and it doesn't
open them.

### See all your memories

**Memories** lists everything from today and from this week in earlier
years. To open it:

- Click **All memories** on the Memories card of any Home.
- Press `⌘K` and type "Memories".
- In the [calendar](./calendar.md), open **On this day** and click **All
  memories**.

<img src="/img/app/memories-dark.png" alt="Memories: what was written on this day a year and two years ago, then this week in earlier years" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

It has two parts: **On this day** and **This week, in earlier years**. Click
a memory to open it. At the bottom, **Your years in writing** lists each year
you wrote in. Click a year to open its story (see below). Press `Esc` to
close Memories.

### A memory when φ opens

Once a day, when you open φ on a day that has a memory, a small card offers
it in the corner of the window. Click it to open **Memories**, or click
**×** to put it away. If you leave it, it goes away by itself.

To stop it, turn off **A memory when φ opens** in **Settings → Appearance →
Moments**.

## Your year in φ Poiesis

<img src="/img/app/year-wrap-dark.png" alt="The first card of Your year in φ Poiesis, filling the window in the theme’s colours" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

**Your year in φ Poiesis** tells the story of a year's writing, one card at a
time:

- How many words you wrote, with a sense of their size (*About the length of
  a novel.*).
- The days you sat down to write, and your longest run of days in a row.
- Your fullest month, the day of the week you wrote most, and the time of day
  you usually began.
- Your journal entries and morning pages.
- Where the work went: the project you wrote most in, new documents, projects
  begun and goals reached.
- Your biggest day.
- How the year began, and where it left off.

<img src="/img/app/year-wrap-days-light.png" alt="A card from Your year in φ Poiesis: the fullest month, with a bar for each month of the year" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />
<img src="/img/app/year-wrap-days-dark.png" alt="A card from Your year in φ Poiesis: the fullest month, with a bar for each month of the year" width="1600" height="1000" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

To move through it, click the card or press `→`. Click its left side or press
`←` to go back. Press `Esc` to leave.

On the last card, **Save as image** saves a picture of the year, and **Done**
closes the story. φ makes the
picture on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

**Where to find it.** From December 1 to January 15, a card on each Home
offers the year that is ending. Click it to begin. Any other time, press
`⌘K` and type the year, or open **Memories** and choose the year under **Your
years in writing**. The year you are in reads *so far*.

## Celebrations

When you reach a milestone, φ plays a short burst of confetti, in the colours
of your theme, with a line that says what you reached. φ celebrates:

- A streak of **7**, **30**, **100** or **365** days.
- **10,000**, **50,000**, **100,000**, **250,000**, **500,000** and
  **1,000,000** words written in φ.
- A book reaching its word goal.
- Your **1st**, **30th** and **100th** morning pages.

Each milestone is celebrated once. What you reached before you had this
version of φ is remembered, without a celebration.

To stop the confetti, turn off **Celebrations** in **Settings → Appearance →
Moments**.

:::note Reduce motion

With **Reduce motion** on in your computer's accessibility settings, there is
no confetti. Only the line shows. The numbers in your year's story also
appear at once instead of counting up.

:::
