# Places, lore & the timeline

**World** keeps the rest of your story's world, beside its
[characters](./characters-and-authors.md). It has three tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Places** | The towns, rooms and roads your story moves through. A place can sit inside another: a room inside a house inside a town. |
| **Lore** | Customs, histories, the rules of your world. |
| **Timeline** | What happens, in the story's own time ("Year 3, spring"), with where it happens and who is there. |

Everything saves as you type and syncs like the rest of the vault.

## Open the world

- In **Write**, click **World** under **World** in the sidebar. This shows
  the world of every project.
- Inside a project, click the **World** tab under the project's name in the
  list (`⌃3`; **Alt+3** on Windows and Linux). Or right-click the project in
  the sidebar and choose **World**. This shows that project's world.
- Press `⌘G` then `L`. Inside a project, it opens the project's World tab;
  anywhere else, the vault's.
- Press `⌘P` and choose **World: places**, **World: lore** or **World:
  timeline**.

The menu at the top of the page, **Every project** or a project's name,
picks whose world it shows.

## Add an entry

1. Choose **Places**, **Lore** or **Timeline** at the top of the page.
2. Press **New place**, **New lore** or **New event**, and type its name.
3. Fill in the entry on the right.

Every entry has a **Name**, **In a line** (a short summary that shows in the
list), **Notes**, and **Belongs to**: **Every project**, or one project, as
with characters. A new entry made in a project's world belongs to that
project.

| Kind | It also has |
| --- | --- |
| Place | **Inside**: the place it sits in, or **Nowhere else (top level)**. **What happens here** lists the timeline's events set in it. |
| Event | **When, in the story**, such as *Year 3, spring* or *the night of the fire*. **Where**: one of your places. **Who's there**: the project's characters. |

The timeline lists events in the order you set, not by date, since the
story keeps its own time. To move an event, open it and use the **Earlier**
and **Later** arrows beside **Order on the timeline**.

## See what a document brings in

In a Write document, open the **Info** panel (`⇧⌘I`) and choose its **World**
tab. It lists:

- The **characters** the document brings in: @-mentioned, or named in the
  text.
- The **places** and **lore** it names.
- The **research** pages linked to it.

Click a character, place or lore entry to see a line about it, and **Open**
to go to its sheet. Click a research page to open it beside the page.

Places and lore can't be linked from the text yet, so φ Poiesis finds them
by their names in the text.

## Delete an entry

Press **Delete** at the foot of the entry. φ asks first. The entry goes to the
[trash](./organizing.md#trash), where you can restore it. Places inside a
deleted place move up a level.
