# Preview

**Preview** shows every page of your book before you export it. You can look
at it as a printed book, as an ebook on a phone or tablet, as a web page, or
as a manuscript. Preview does not save any file. You can change a setting and
look again as often as you like.

## Open the preview

1. Open the project's **Export** page (see
   [How exporting works](./exporting)).
2. Press **Preview**, at the top of the page.
3. In the toolbar, choose what to look at: **Print**, **Ebook**, **Web page**
   or **Manuscript**.

The preview uses the choices you made on the Export page: the design, the
trim size (the size of the printed page), the paper and your adjustments.

## Looking through a printed book

- **Spreads** shows two facing pages, as you see them in an open book. The
  cover is shown alone. After it, left and right pages are shown together.
- **Single pages** shows one page at a time.
- Use the arrows to go to the next or previous page.
- Click the page number to go to a page you choose.
- **Zoom in** (**+**) and **Zoom out** (**−**) change the size. The pages
  stay sharp at any size.

Pages appear quickly at first and become sharp a moment later.

## The ebook

**Ebook** shows the EPUB page by page, as a reading app does. Choose
**Phone**, **Tablet** or **Screen** to see it at that size.

Readers choose their own text size, so the book on their screen will not
look exactly like your preview. Check these things:

- how chapters open;
- the ornaments;
- the book's own pages, such as the title page and the dedication.
