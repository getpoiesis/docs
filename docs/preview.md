# Preview

**Preview** shows the whole book as it will come out: as a printed book in
spreads, as an ebook on a phone or tablet, as a web page, or as a manuscript.
Nothing is saved; change a setting and look again.

## Open the preview

1. Open the project's **Export** page.
2. Press **Preview**, at the top of the page.
3. Choose what to look at in the toolbar: **Print**, **Ebook**, **Web page** or
   **Manuscript**.

The preview follows what's chosen on the Export page: the design, the trim
size, the paper and your adjustments.

## Looking through a printed book

- **Spreads** shows facing pages, as the bound book opens: the cover alone,
  then left and right pages together. **Single pages** shows one at a time.
- The arrows step through the book; click the page number to jump to a page.
- **Zoom in** and **Zoom out** (the **+** and **−**) change the size; the
  pages stay sharp at any zoom.

Pages appear quickly at first and sharpen a moment later.

## The ebook

**Ebook** pages through the EPUB as a reading app would, on a **Phone**, a
**Tablet** or a **Screen**. Readers choose their own type size, so their
screens will differ from yours; what to look at is how chapters open, the
ornaments, and the book's own pages.
