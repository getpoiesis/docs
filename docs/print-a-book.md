# Print a book

The **Print book** tab makes the two files a print-on-demand service asks for:
the interior, as a print-ready PDF, and the cover, front, spine and back on one
page. φ works out the margins, the blank pages before chapters, and the spine
from the page count.

## Make the files

1. Open the project's **Export** page and choose **Print book**.
2. Choose the four things a print service asks for:
   - **Design**: how the book is set (see [Designs](./designs)).
   - **Trim size**: the size of the printed page.
   - **Paper**: **White** or **Cream**.
   - **Ink**: **Black**, or keep φ's colour.
3. Read the check below the choices, and fix anything it lists.
4. Press **Export print PDF** for the interior.
5. Press **Export cover** for the full-wrap cover.
6. Upload the two files to your print service separately.

## Choosing the trim size

| Trim size | Usual for |
| --- | --- |
| **Pocket — 5 × 8 in** | Mass-market paperbacks |
| **5.25 × 8 in** | Shorter novels |
| **Digest — 5.5 × 8.5 in** | Novels, memoir, poetry |
| **Trade — 6 × 9 in** | Most fiction and non-fiction; the safe choice |
| **Royal — 6.14 × 9.21 in** | Larger non-fiction |
| **7 × 10 in** | Workbooks and illustrated books |
| **Letter — 8.5 × 11 in** | Manuals and large-format books |

These are the sizes KDP and IngramSpark print. If a project is set to a paper
they don't print (A4, say), the tab says so and asks you to choose one.

## Paper and ink

**Cream** is usual for fiction and **White** for non-fiction. Cream is
slightly thicker, so the same book has a wider spine.

Links and citations print in black by default, so no screen colour reaches a
printed page. Pictures keep their colour either way. Choose **Keep φ's
colour** only if you're paying for colour printing.

## What φ takes care of

- **Chapters open on a right-hand page.** φ adds the blank page before them
  when a design asks for it. You can turn this off under **Adjust the design**.
- **Margins for the binding.** The inside margin grows with the page count, as
  print services require, so the text clears the spine.
- **Page count limits.** A paperback needs at least 24 pages and holds at most
  828; the check tells you if the book falls outside that.
- **No cover inside the interior.** The cover is a file of its own.
- **Footnotes at the foot of their page**, and running heads and page numbers
  as the design sets them, with none on chapter openings.

## The cover

**Export cover** makes one PDF with the back, the spine and the front, plus
the eighth of an inch of bleed print services trim away.

- **The front** is your cover art, set at the top of the project's page.
- **The back and spine** take their colour from the cover art. The back
  carries the book's description and the first author's short bio, and leaves
  the corner where the barcode goes clear.
- **The spine** is sized from the page count and the paper. From 80 pages on,
  it carries the title and author; below that it's too narrow, and stays plain.

The check warns when the cover art is too small to print sharply: a printed
cover needs about 300 pixels per inch.

:::tip Order a proof copy

Before you publish, order one printed proof from your print service. It's the
only way to see the colours, the margins and the spine as a reader will.

:::
